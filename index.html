Circuit Sort - Screw Puzzle

Circuit Sort - Screw Puzzle is a casual mobile puzzle game. Unscrew bolts, sort them into the right bins, and clear each circuit board without getting jammed.

What this app is for

  • Short puzzle sessions on your phone
  • Campaign levels, daily challenges, and arcade-style runs
  • Optional Google sign-in for cloud backup of progress on supported builds

This site is the public home page for the Circuit Sort - Screw Puzzle app (package com.circuitsort.screwpuzzle), developed by Brock Hall.
